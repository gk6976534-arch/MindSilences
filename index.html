MindSilence™ is marketed as a dietary supplement containing a combination of vitamins, minerals, botanical extracts, amino acids, and probiotic ingredients. Current product-related sources list the following seven active ingredients:

Vitamin B6

Vitamin B6, listed as pyridoxal 5′-phosphate, supports normal nervous-system function and plays a role in neurotransmitter production. The reviewed label lists 1.2 mg per capsule.
2

Zinc

Zinc is an essential mineral involved in normal immune function, metabolism, and neurological processes. MindSilence™ lists 2.5 mg of zinc as zinc citrate per capsule.
3

Ginkgo Biloba Extract

Ginkgo biloba is a botanical extract commonly used in supplements formulated for cognitive and circulatory support. The MindSilence™ label lists 150 mg of leaf extract.
4

Probiotic Blend

The formula includes a probiotic blend containing several Lactobacillus strains, including L. paracasei, L. buchneri, L. brevis, and L. hilgardii. The reviewed label lists the blend at 150 mg.
5

Taurine

Taurine is an amino acid naturally present in the body and involved in several physiological processes, including nervous-system function. The MindSilence™ label lists 150 mg per capsule.
6

Passion Flower

Passion flower is a traditional botanical commonly included in relaxation-oriented supplements. MindSilence™ lists 75 mg of passion flower powder, sourced from the stem, leaves, and flowers.
7

Valerian Extract

Valerian root has a long history of use in traditional herbal preparations for relaxation and sleep support. The reviewed MindSilence™ label lists 75 mg of valerian root extract.

Other Ingredients
The reviewed label also lists hypromellose (vegetable capsule), microcrystalline cellulose, and olive oil as other ingredients.
